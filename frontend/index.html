Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to expand the answer.

Why is a video not playing?

Try switching to a different server using the server selector below the player. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache or try a different browser.

How do I add something to Watch Later?

Click the bookmark icon on any movie or TV show card to save it to your Watch Later list.

Can I download movies or episodes?

Download options may be available for some titles or episodes. If a download button is shown, follow the available instructions and make sure you have the necessary rights to download the content.

Why do some episodes have no video?

Some episodes may not have a working stream available. Try another server if one is provided, or report the missing video through our support channel.

How do I change the video server?

Open the movie or series player and use the server selector below the video player to choose another available server.
